Privacy Notice

Registration Information

The event registration system collects only the information needed to manage participant registration, attendance, accessibility assistance, event entitlements, and event-related records.

Information may include your name, mobile number, city/province, participant type, companion relationship, requested assistance, emergency contact details, Senior Citizen/PWD status, authorization status, and registration consent.

Biometric privacy: fingerprint or face biometric data remains on the participant’s own device. This event system is designed not to store fingerprint images, face images, or raw biometric templates.

Event staff should access registration information only for event administration and verification purposes.

This prototype currently stores registration records in the browser on the device being used. A future shared online registration database should use appropriate access controls, retention rules, and data-protection procedures.
